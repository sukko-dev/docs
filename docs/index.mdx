# Sukko Documentation

Sukko is **realtime infrastructure for the part that's hard to get right** — staying lossless when a node fails or a consumer group rebalances, proven on exactly that in a public fault-matrix benchmark. Deploy it in your own cloud, connect your Kafka/Redpanda pipeline, and deliver real-time data to thousands of clients — with nothing leaving your infrastructure.
